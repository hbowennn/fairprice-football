How the estimates work

Match probabilities are de-vigged. Expected goals are fitted to your 1X2 and over/under 2.5 odds using a simple Poisson model. Player stats are entered as historical totals and converted to per-90 rates. When xG is supplied, the goal rate starts from xG/90 and blends in actual goals/90 with a conservative weight that rises with historical minutes and is capped at 25%. If xG is left blank, the model falls back to actual goals/90 instead of treating missing xG as zero. Entering 0 is treated as a real xG value. The app calculates expected minutes as starting probability × minutes if starting + substitute probability × minutes if a substitute. The team expected-goals environment makes a restrained adjustment to the player goal rate. Shot and shots-on-target rates are modelled separately. The optional penalty xG/90 input is added to the goal rate; the penalty-taker checkbox is only a label and does not itself change the calculation.

Each market shows fair odds and the target price for 5% expected value (1.05 divided by estimated probability). First-goalscorer remains a rough approximation and is less reliable when only some likely scorers are entered.

Important: these estimates are not calibrated or guaranteed profitable. The finishing adjustment and team scaling are initial heuristics; validate them against historical results before relying on the prices.
