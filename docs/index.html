How the estimates work

Match probabilities are de-vigged. Expected goals are fitted to your 1X2 and over/under 2.5 odds using a simple Poisson model. Player estimates use your per-90 rates, expected minutes and starting chance. 2+ goals, hattrick, 2+ shots, 3+ shots and 2+ shots on target use Poisson probabilities. Each market shows fair odds and the minimum decimal price corresponding to 5% expected value (1.05 divided by estimated probability). First-goalscorer is a rough estimate based on the player’s modelled goal rate relative to expected match goals; it is less reliable when only some players are entered. The 5% EV price is a model threshold, not a guarantee of profit.

Important: these are baseline estimates, not guaranteed value. Use reliable stats and test the model against historical results before relying on it.
