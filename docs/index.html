How the estimates work

Match probabilities are de-vigged. Expected goals are fitted to your 1X2 and over/under 2.5 odds using a simple Poisson model. Player stats are entered as historical totals and converted to per-90 rates. When xG is supplied, the goal rate starts from xG/90 and blends in actual goals/90 with a conservative weight that rises with historical minutes and is capped at 25%. If xG is left blank, the model falls back to actual goals/90 instead of treating missing xG as zero. Entering 0 is treated as a real xG value. The app calculates expected minutes as starting probability × minutes if starting + substitute probability × minutes if a substitute. The team expected-goals environment makes a restrained adjustment to the player goal rate. Shot and shots-on-target rates are modelled separately. Team attacking xG/90 and defending xGA/90 are also entered for both sides. For each team’s players, the model combines that team’s attacking xG/90 with the opponent’s defending xGA/90, relative to the league average xGA baseline, using a geometric-mean factor capped at ±15%. Blank values are treated as league average. This is an additional modest adjustment, not a replacement for match odds. Opponent defence is also applied using manually entered xGA, shots allowed and shots-on-target allowed per match. Each statistic is shrunk towards its league average with an 8-match prior; goal effects are capped at ±10%, while shot and SOT effects are capped at ±15%. Blank opponent stats mean a neutral league-average adjustment. Goal adjustments are modest because match odds already reflect some defensive strength. If the penalty-taker box is ticked, the model adds its generic penalty allowance, scaled by expected minutes. These are initial assumptions, not league-calibrated rates.

Each market shows fair odds and the target price for 5% expected value (1.05 divided by estimated probability). First-goalscorer remains a rough approximation and is less reliable when only some likely scorers are entered.

Important: these estimates are not calibrated or guaranteed profitable. The finishing adjustment and team scaling are initial heuristics; validate them against historical results before relying on the prices.
