⚽ FairPrice Football

Enter match odds, estimate team goals, then build player prices using minutes and player rates. No Betfair connection required.

1. Match and market odds

Enter decimal odds from a bookmaker or exchange. Use the same market source for each row.

2. Player model

Use season rates from a free stats source where available, then override likely starts and minutes yourself. The app does not assume an unconfirmed lineup is correct.

3. Free data helper (optional)

football-data.org offers a free tier for fixtures, results, tables and some lineups. It does not provide bookmaker odds on its free tier or comprehensive player shot rates. You can use the data as a reference, then manually override the lineup/minutes above.

Get a free token at football-data.org registration. API coverage and free limits vary; no token is required for manual modelling.

How to use the estimates

Odds are decimal. Match probabilities are de-vigged. Team expected goals are a simple independent-Poisson fit to your 1X2 and O/U 2.5 prices. Player estimates use your entered per-90 rates, minutes and start probability, scaled to the modelled team goal environment.

Important: this is a baseline model, not a guaranteed edge. Player prices depend on role, penalties, opposition, lineups and data quality. Compare to current prices and paper-test before risking money.
